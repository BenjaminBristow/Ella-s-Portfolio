A Bit About Me

I’m Ella—a creative and super enthusiastic Advertising and Digital Marketing student at Lancaster University. I genuinely believe in the power of marketing to tell great stories, build real connections with people, and bring wild ideas to life.

My sweet spot is anywhere involving sport, automotive, film, television, or theatre. I love figuring out how raw creativity and solid strategy click together to build memorable campaigns.

Whether I'm managing social channels, filming short-form video content, or promoting live stage productions, I'm always looking for ways to understand audiences better and bring a fresh perspective to the table.
