A BIT ABOUT ME

I’m Ella, a creative and enthusiastic Advertising and Digital Marketing student at Lancaster University, passionate about the power of marketing to tell stories, connect with audiences and bring ideas to life.

With a particular interest in the sport, automotive, film, television and theatre industries, I love exploring how creativity and strategy come together to build memorable campaigns and meaningful brand experiences.

Through my experience in social media marketing, content creation and promoting live theatre, I’m developing my skills in creating engaging content, understanding audiences and bringing fresh perspectives to every project.

I’m a hardworking, reliable and positive person who loves taking on new challenges, collaborating with others and thinking outside the box. I’m excited to gain hands-on industry experience as I work towards a career in creative marketing and advertising.
